Ella Simeon · Portfolio case study

Airline Analytics: From Booking to Capacity

Two decisions across the passenger journey: whether a flight-search redesign improves booking conversion, and where observed route traffic suggests a closer look at seat capacity.

01 · Product experimentation

Flight Search A/B Test

A simulated booking funnel shows how to evaluate a redesign. Treatment converted at 8.73% versus 8.23% for control, but the difference was inconclusive (p = 0.172).

02 · Commercial forecasting

Route Demand & Capacity

Public BTS T-100 traffic data powers a tested forecast for six nonstop routes and a hypothetical seat planning scenario. Forecast error stays visible alongside the recommendation.

The booking experiment uses simulated data. It is not a JetBlue experiment and does not contain customer records.
