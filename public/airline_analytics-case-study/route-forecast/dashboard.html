Decision context

The baseline repeats passenger counts from the same month one year earlier. A seasonal growth variant applies a capped year-over-year factor. The lower-error method on a 2024 validation year is selected for each route; 2025 remains untouched until the final holdout evaluation.

Operational decisions would also need current seat schedules, fares, revenue, competitive supply, and margin. The BTS counts describe passengers carried, which can be constrained by seats offered; they are not an unconstrained demand estimate.

Data lineage: Bureau of Transportation Statistics T-100 scheduled passenger segments, accessed via the processed route_carrier_month release of the independent T100 Route Explorer project. Six domestic directional routes only; 2022–April 2026. Analysis by Ella Simeon.
